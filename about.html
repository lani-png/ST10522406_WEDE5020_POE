About OffGrid Apparel

OffGrid Apparel is a local clothing retailer offering affordable and modern clothing for people who want to express their own personal style.

Our collections are inspired by modern streetwear and Y2K fashion, combining comfortable everyday clothing with unique styles.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide affordable, stylish and accessible clothing while encouraging customers to express their individuality.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted local clothing retailer known for affordable fashion, quality products and unique styles.

Why Choose OffGrid Apparel?

  • Affordable clothing
  • Modern Y2K-inspired styles
  • Quality products
  • Wide range of clothing and accessories
  • Local South African brand
